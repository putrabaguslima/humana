3. Information Handled by the Application

A. On-Device Local Storage (Apple SwiftData)

The App allows users to bookmark favorite healthcare providers, save frequently checked medications to an offline Medicine Cabinet, and record local reminder preferences.

  • All bookmark data is stored purely on your physical iPhone or iPad using Apple's native SwiftData framework.
  • This data remains strictly isolated in your device sandbox. We do not maintain any cloud databases, backends, or servers to sync or harvest your bookmarks.
  • You can delete your bookmarks at any time directly within the App, or uninstall the App to permanently erase all locally cached records.

B. Location Services (CoreLocation & MapKit)

When you search for healthcare providers or calculate driving distance, the App may request permission to access your location ("While Using the App"):

  • On-Device Geocoding: Your GPS coordinate is processed locally via Apple MapKit to determine your general city/state and calculate approximate driving mileage to medical clinics.
  • No Location Tracking or History: We do not track, log, profile, transmit, or sell your geographic movements or real-time location.
  • Revocable Anytime: Location access is entirely optional. You can deny or revoke location access at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Humana Medicare, and manually enter a ZIP code instead.

C. Public Government API Queries

To provide up-to-date information, the App communicates with two free public U.S. Government APIs:

  • CMS NPPES NPI Registry API: https://npiregistry.cms.hhs.gov/api/?version=2.1 — Queries doctor names, taxonomy specialties, and postal codes. No personal user identification is transmitted.
  • FDA openFDA Drug Label API: https://api.fda.gov/drug/label.json — Queries generic and brand drug labels, usage indications, and safety warnings.

Both government endpoints are queried over secure, encrypted Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2+ / HTTPS). No cookies, user tokens, or account credentials are exchanged.

D. Local Device Notifications

The App provides optional local notifications for Medicare Annual Enrollment Period (AEP) deadlines and personal prescription reminder schedules. Notifications are scheduled entirely on your local iOS system via the UserNotifications framework. No external push notification servers or cloud tracking services are utilized.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdMob

The App displays non-intrusive advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to support free informational access. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device information, such as:

  • Device identifiers (IDFV, and IDFA only if you grant explicit permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt).
  • IP address (used to infer coarse geographic region for ad serving).
  • Ad interaction and performance metrics (impressions, clicks, diagnostic crash logs).

All ad formats within the App strictly comply with Google AdMob Terms of Service (TOS):

  • App Open Ads: Governed by strict frequency capping (minimum 300-second interval) to prevent disruption during app warm starts.
  • Interstitial Ads: Displayed only at natural workflow transition points with an enforced 60-second cooldown period to prevent spam.
  • Rewarded Ads: 100% voluntary opt-in. Users are given explicit notice before watching short sponsor videos to unlock features like PDF cost summary reports.

The contents of the App—including Medicare guide summaries, baseline copay calculations, out-of-pocket estimations, and FDA drug directory information—are provided solely for educational and informational reference.

Humana Medicare is designed for seniors, caregivers, and adults navigating Medicare coverage. The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & California CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have certain legal rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access & Portability: All personal bookmarks and preferences exist locally on your device and are directly accessible within the App interface.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: You can wipe all stored data at any time by removing saved providers and medications or by deleting the App from your device.
  • Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
  • Opting Out of Targeted Advertising: You can limit personalized advertising by adjusting your iOS settings under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking or Apple Advertising.

8. Security Safeguards

We employ industry-standard security practices to protect all data flows:

  • Mandatory TLS 1.2+ encryption on all public network communications via native iOS URLSession.
  • Local data isolation within iOS sandboxed file containers.
  • Zero transmission of unencrypted plaintext sensitive metrics.
